What Is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment focuses on correcting the position of teeth and jaws. It can help address common concerns such as overcrowded teeth, gaps between teeth, overbites, underbites, and other alignment problems.

When teeth are properly aligned, they may be easier to clean and maintain. Correcting certain bite problems can also improve chewing function and reduce uneven pressure on teeth. An orthodontist evaluates your teeth, bite, and jaw alignment to recommend a treatment plan based on your individual needs.

Types of Orthodontic Treatment in Hixson, TN

Different orthodontic solutions are available depending on the patient's age, dental condition, lifestyle, and treatment goals.

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are a reliable option for correcting many types of tooth alignment and bite problems. They use brackets and wires to apply controlled pressure that gradually moves teeth into better positions.

Metal braces can be suitable for children, teenagers, and adults. Regular orthodontic appointments allow the orthodontist to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments throughout treatment.

Clear Braces

Clear or ceramic braces work similarly to traditional braces but use tooth-colored brackets that blend more naturally with the teeth. They may appeal to patients who want an orthodontic option that is less noticeable.

An orthodontic consultation can help determine whether clear braces are appropriate for your alignment needs and daily routine.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually move teeth. Because the aligners can be removed for meals, brushing, and flossing, they offer flexibility for many patients.

Clear aligners may be suitable for certain mild to complex alignment concerns, although eligibility depends on the individual case. Your orthodontist can determine whether Invisalign is an appropriate choice for your smile.

Early Orthodontic Treatment for Children

Children's teeth and jaws develop as they grow, making early orthodontic evaluations helpful for identifying potential alignment problems. An orthodontist may recommend an initial evaluation around age seven to check how permanent teeth are developing and whether the bite needs monitoring.

Benefits of Straightening Your Teeth

Orthodontic treatment can provide several benefits beyond appearance. Properly aligned teeth may be easier to brush and floss, helping patients maintain a consistent oral hygiene routine.

Correcting bite problems may also improve how the upper and lower teeth meet when chewing. For some patients, orthodontic care can reduce issues associated with uneven tooth contact and improve overall bite function.

A more balanced smile may also help people feel more confident when speaking, smiling, and interacting with others. However, treatment results vary, and an orthodontist should evaluate individual concerns before recommending a specific solution.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

The length of orthodontic treatment depends on several factors, including the severity of the alignment problem, the type of appliance used, and how consistently the patient follows the orthodontist's instructions.

Some cases may take less time, while more complex bite and alignment concerns can require longer treatment. Attending scheduled appointments and following instructions about braces or aligners can help keep treatment on track.

Your orthodontist will provide an estimated timeline based on your specific condition and monitor your progress throughout the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child see an orthodontist?

An initial orthodontic evaluation around age seven is commonly recommended. The orthodontist can assess dental development and decide whether treatment or future monitoring is appropriate.

Are braces better than Invisalign?

Neither option is best for everyone. Braces can address many alignment and bite concerns, while Invisalign offers removable, clear aligners for suitable cases. An orthodontist can recommend an option based on your dental needs.

Is orthodontic treatment only for cosmetic reasons?

No. Orthodontic treatment can improve tooth alignment and bite function as well as smile appearance. Properly aligned teeth may also be easier to clean.
